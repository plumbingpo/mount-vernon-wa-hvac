Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and expensive water damage from clogged condensate lines. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Our commercial installations and inspections cover heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Maintenance Programs

Proactive HVAC Care for Businesses

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment is preventing failures before they happen. Mount Vernon HVAC offers customized scheduled maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, coil cleaning and condensate line clearing, filter changes with MERV ratings matched to the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant charge and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal preparation for peak demand throughout the year. Each program is tailored to the property's specific equipment, usage patterns, and budget requirements.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints.
